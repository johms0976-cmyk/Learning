# Recording list

Every file is a short **.mp3**. Record at a calm, slightly slow pace —
these are for a child who is sounding words out.

Any file you have not recorded yet is simply read by the computer voice,
so you can record them in any order and drop them in as you go.

| what | where it goes |
|---|---|
| letter sounds | `audio/wordland/sounds/` |
| words | `audio/wordland/words/` |
| story chapters | `audio/wordland/story/` |

Map 1's chapters keep their original names (`chapter1.mp3` …), so
anything you recorded before still plays. Later maps are prefixed:
`map2-chapter1.mp3`.

## 3 · Story chapters — 20 files

Read each chapter as one take. The words in the story are also tappable,
and those play the single-word files above.

### Map 1 · The Lost Letters

#### `story/chapter1.mp3` — Zib Lands in the Moss
*Mossy Meadow*

> The wind blew Zib into the moss.
> A monkey and a mouse were hiding there.
> 'Mmm,' said the monkey. 'That is my sound!'
> Zib put M in the bag. One letter home.

#### `story/chapter2.mp3` — The Sound in the Sand
*Silver Sands*

> Zib walked out onto the sand.
> The sun was hot. A snail went slow.
> A snake hissed, 'Sss, that one is mine!'
> Now Zib had M and S.

#### `story/chapter3.mp3` — A Boy Called Sam
*Apple Hollow*

> In Apple Hollow, Zib met a boy.
> 'I am Sam,' said the boy.
> Sam had jam on his chin!
> A and I jumped into the bag.

#### `story/chapter4.mp3` — Ten Tall Trees
*Tall Tree Trail*

> Sam and Zib walked the tall tree trail.
> A tiger slept beside a tent.
> Tuh, tuh, went the little train.
> T was found. Four letters now!

#### `story/chapter5.mp3` — The Cat on the Mat
*Cat Cave*

> Deep in the cave, a cat sat on a mat.
> A bat and a rat sat down too.
> 'We all end the same,' said the cat. '-at!'
> Bat, cat, hat, mat, rat, sat.

#### `story/chapter6.mp3` — Bees on the Bridge
*Bumble Bridge*

> A wooden bridge buzzed with bees.
> A bear wanted the bread. So did the bees!
> 'Buh, buh, bear,' laughed Sam.
> B went into the bag.

#### `story/chapter7.mp3` — A Crab in a Cup
*Cloud Cove*

> The cove was full of soft clouds.
> A crab was fast asleep in a cup!
> A cow drove past in a car. 'Kuh!'
> C was safe in the bag.

#### `story/chapter8.mp3` — The Fox by the Fire
*Fox Forest*

> A fox sat by a warm fire.
> A fish and a frog sat down too.
> 'Fff,' said the fire. 'That is my sound.'
> F was the seventh letter.

#### `story/chapter9.mp3` — Camp of Words
*Sam's Camp*

> At camp, Sam made words in the sand.
> I am Sam. A cat sat.
> Bat, mat, fat, hat — all of them!
> One place left. The tower is close.

#### `story/chapter10.mp3` — The Spellbook Opens
*Wizard's Tower*

> At the top of the tower lay the big book.
> Zib tipped out M, S, A, T, B, C, F, I.
> The book glowed. Words came back to Word Land!
> 'More letters are lost,' said Zib. 'Ready?'

### Map 2 · Words That Hold Hands

#### `story/map2-chapter1.mp3` — The Nest on the Ridge
*Nest Ridge*

> Beyond the tower, a high ridge climbed into the sky.
> Nine eggs sat in one round nest.
> 'Nnn,' hummed the bird. 'Nest. Nose. Nut.'
> I can see it, said Zib. N was letter nine.

#### `story/map2-chapter2.mp3` — A Pig on the Peach Path
*Peach Path*

> The path down the ridge was lined with peach trees.
> A pig was eating a pear off a plate!
> 'Puh, puh, pig,' said Sam, and gave it a pat.
> P was in the bag. 'I am quick,' said Zib.

#### `story/map2-chapter3.mp3` — The Market of Maps
*Map Market*

> Every stall in the market sold one thing: maps.
> A man in a red cap filled a cup at the tap.
> 'They all end the same,' said Sam. '-ap!'
> Cap, gap, lap, map, nap, tap, zap.

#### `story/map2-chapter4.mp3` — The Horse on Hammer Hill
*Hammer Hill*

> Hammer Hill rang all day: tap, tap, tap.
> A horse in a helmet was building a hive.
> 'Huh, huh, hat,' it puffed, and lost its hat.
> Sam caught it. H was theirs.

#### `story/map2-chapter5.mp3` — A Rope Across the Ridge
*Rope Ridge*

> One long rope crossed the gap between two cliffs.
> A rabbit held a rose. A robot held a ruler.
> 'Rrr,' growled the rope as they pulled.
> A rat ran over first. R was letter twelve.

#### `story/map2-chapter6.mp3` — The Van in the Valley
*Van Valley*

> Down in the valley an old van was stuck in the mud.
> A man with a fan and a pan came to help.
> 'Can we?' said Sam. They pushed. The van ran!
> Can, fan, man, pan, ran, tan, van.

#### `story/map2-chapter7.mp3` — The Zebra's Zigzag
*Zigzag Zoo*

> The path through the zoo went zigzag, left and right.
> A zebra with a zip on its coat led the way.
> 'Zzz,' it buzzed. 'Zap! Zero! Zoo!'
> Sam laughed. Z went into the bag.

#### `story/map2-chapter8.mp3` — Three Bees in the Tree
*Bee Tree*

> One huge tree stood alone, humming.
> Three bees flew out. Then a sheep and a queen!
> 'Eee,' sang the bees. 'That is two e's, together.'
> 'I can see it,' said Sam. 'Bee. Tree. See.'

#### `story/map2-chapter9.mp3` — Words That Hold Hands
*Sentence Springs*

> At the springs, the words were floating in the water.
> Sam caught three and put them in a row.
> I can see a cat. It was a whole sentence!
> 'Words hold hands,' said Zib. 'That is reading.'

#### `story/map2-chapter10.mp3` — The Light at the Top
*Lantern Lighthouse*

> The lighthouse leaned out over a dark, wide sea.
> Zib emptied the bag: N, P, H, R, Z and E.
> The lamp lit. Across the water, sentences appeared.
> 'The man ran,' read Sam — every word, all by himself.
