# Recording list

Every file is a short **.mp3**. Record at a calm, slightly slow pace —
these are for a child who is sounding words out.

Any file you have not recorded yet is simply read by the computer voice,
so you can record them in any order and drop them in as you go.

| what | where it goes |
|---|---|
| letter sounds | `audio/wordland/sounds/` |
| words | `audio/wordland/words/` |
| story chapters | `audio/wordland/story/` |

## 3 · Story chapters — 10 files

Read each chapter as one take. The words in the story are also tappable,
and those play the single-word files above.

### `story/chapter1.mp3` — Zib Lands in the Moss
*Mossy Meadow*

> The wind blew Zib into the moss.
> A monkey and a mouse were hiding there.
> 'Mmm,' said the monkey. 'That is my sound!'
> Zib put M in the bag. One letter home.

### `story/chapter2.mp3` — The Sound in the Sand
*Silver Sands*

> Zib walked out onto the sand.
> The sun was hot. A snail went slow.
> A snake hissed, 'Sss, that one is mine!'
> Now Zib had M and S.

### `story/chapter3.mp3` — A Boy Called Sam
*Apple Hollow*

> In Apple Hollow, Zib met a boy.
> 'I am Sam,' said the boy.
> Sam had jam on his chin!
> A and I jumped into the bag.

### `story/chapter4.mp3` — Ten Tall Trees
*Tall Tree Trail*

> Sam and Zib walked the tall tree trail.
> A tiger slept beside a tent.
> Tuh, tuh, went the little train.
> T was found. Four letters now!

### `story/chapter5.mp3` — The Cat on the Mat
*Cat Cave*

> Deep in the cave, a cat sat on a mat.
> A bat and a rat sat down too.
> 'We all end the same,' said the cat. '-at!'
> Bat, cat, hat, mat, rat, sat.

### `story/chapter6.mp3` — Bees on the Bridge
*Bumble Bridge*

> A wooden bridge buzzed with bees.
> A bear wanted the bread. So did the bees!
> 'Buh, buh, bear,' laughed Sam.
> B went into the bag.

### `story/chapter7.mp3` — A Crab in a Cup
*Cloud Cove*

> The cove was full of soft clouds.
> A crab was fast asleep in a cup!
> A cow drove past in a car. 'Kuh!'
> C was safe in the bag.

### `story/chapter8.mp3` — The Fox by the Fire
*Fox Forest*

> A fox sat by a warm fire.
> A fish and a frog sat down too.
> 'Fff,' said the fire. 'That is my sound.'
> F was the seventh letter.

### `story/chapter9.mp3` — Camp of Words
*Sam's Camp*

> At camp, Sam made words in the sand.
> I am Sam. A cat sat.
> Bat, mat, fat, hat — all of them!
> One place left. The tower is close.

### `story/chapter10.mp3` — The Spellbook Opens
*Wizard's Tower*

> At the top of the tower lay the big book.
> Zib tipped out M, S, A, T, B, C, F, I.
> The book glowed. Words came back to Word Land!
> 'More letters are lost,' said Zib. 'Ready?'
